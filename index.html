The Fifth Why

Ask why five times. The engineering and business decisions behind history's biggest failures.

The Fifth Why is an educational YouTube channel. Each episode examines an engineering disaster, a corporate collapse or a market bubble, using primary sources such as official investigation reports, court filings and annual reports.

This site also hosts the information page for explainer-factory, the private tool the channel owner uses to upload videos to their own YouTube channel.
